This is the nineteenth-century printed Franko edition, **not** an image of the ancient text or a verified primary manuscript. The [frozen first native-source-image-only reading](../pilot/vol1_scan00038_single_reading.json) records **41 complete physical body-line positions**, plus one observed folio. Eleven numbered items, source paragraphs of **3 + 2 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 1 + 5 + 12 + 3 + 3** lines, cover titles **16–26**: Exodus/Assumption of Moses, Eldad and Modad, Psalms of Solomon, Songs of David, apocalypses of Elijah, Zephaniah and Zechariah, vision of Isaiah, Jeremiah's Paralipomena, Solomon's Judgments, and Solomon with Kitovras. The final physical line begins a quotation `„все лгано — кажеть ся там` which remains **open** at the page boundary; do not invent its continuation from I/39. No distinct printer colophon or footnote was observed.

The reading retains printed source line breaks, line-end hyphens (`Ма-`/`теріялы` across physical lines in the Sokolov bibliography), original title **letterspacing** and unusual historical Cyrillic characters `ѣ/ъ/і`. The polytonic Greek label `Ἀνάληψις` on body line 2 has a `grc/Grek` span with `uncertain=true` because its exact print breathing/accents have *not* been independently identified. Latin-script Roman centuries/volumes are kept distinct from Cyrillic running text. All line/region boxes are **estimated**, not measured glyph geometry; the full source identity is protected by SHA256.

## Failure-oriented post-freeze challenge

After the first source-only response was persisted, an adversarial visual reread of the original image found a probable meaning-bearing substitution in **body line 17** (entry 21, Apocalypse of Zephaniah): the first reader wrote `переложене.`, but the source appears to print **`перекладене.`**. The frozen first reading remains intact and the candidate correction is anchored in the separate review JSON as **provisional**, not verified.
