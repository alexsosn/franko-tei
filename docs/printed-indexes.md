Section boundaries follow previous contents research and draft evidence, not new image collation. Partitions in web/printed_indexes.py explicitly specify inclusive region end-line ordinals against exact blob SHA and reader ID. Source paths, blob, asset commit, region and literal line IDs/text are retained in the generated projection. A missing or changed blob suppresses the affected page, or both pages of a continued entry, rather than silently reusing obsolete boundaries. The source scans remain accessible.

The entry ID identifies a specific printed entry starting at a physical line; it is not an authority ID. Display labels join physical lines with spaces, without silently normalizing hyphenation. Reference parsing is restricted to manually segmented entries: trailing numeric lists/ranges in names, works and motifs, leading page number in glossary, plus explicitly labelled variant page references in this pilot. Original lines remain intact. Dittos are literal; the clearly identified Abraham motif group and manually identified name contexts receive separate editorial context. See-references are readable literal text; automatic entry resolution is deferred. Citation role, original numeric token, target volume and individually resolved/missing pages are separate. Unique explicit printed folio observations in existing body drafts are used for page links; duplicates, missing observations and invalid numbers are not inferred from scan offsets. They are provisional page destinations, not actual mention matches or verified line anchors.

Build with the existing web/build_search.py command. The resulting search/printed-indexes.json is a derived snapshot, not a second edition or a standalone source of truth. Lazy loading preserves the existing contents dialog and selected reading ref. No new global controls or backend. The partial coverage indicator distinguishes indexed/structured pages from page transcription. The glossary has all four pages structured, the names index has all fifteen pages structured, the works index has all six pages structured, and motifs has its first page. Paired forms in two glossary articles retain both page references and their individual form associations. Other volumes will need source-backed data and cross-volume reference support before publishing. TEI/TF integration remains #239.

Tests: web/test_printed_indexes.py tests exact lines against source candidates, continuations, multiple citations, ditto context, glossary variant references, unique observed folios, absent/changed source and browser behavior. Do not claim fresh image verification or historical accuracy from these tests. The source scans were not accessible visually in the current tool environment; this PR is a data/code pilot, not a new image-reading batch.
